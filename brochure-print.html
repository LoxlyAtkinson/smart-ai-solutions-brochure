Loxly Atkinson

Founder · Smart AI Solutions · Cape Town

I am not a consultant who read about business. I sold and implemented point-of-sale systems into the South African trade for three years, so I know what a real shop floor looks like on a Saturday. Then I spent six years building the software that fixes what POS was never going to solve.

Before I wrote a line of AI code I spent three years as Business Development Manager at IQ Retail's POS division in Stellenbosch, running multi-client implementations and training partners across the reseller network. Before that I was Regional Manager at Corex Partnertech, the ICT distributor, working with resellers, service providers and managed service providers. Two promotions in four years.

In 2020 I left, because I had seen the gap from the inside. I had spent years watching businesses fight the same problems that a till system was never going to solve. Not stock counting. Customers. Follow-up. The hundred small conversations a day that nobody has time for. The tools to fix that did not exist yet, so I went and built them.

Our story

Smart AI Solutions was founded on a simple observation: South African businesses did not need another AI vendor. They needed a partner who would look under the bonnet first.

The practice grew out of years spent inside the IT distribution and point-of-sale trade, where the founders watched good businesses buy good software and still lose money in the gaps between systems. Today we work across education, broadcasting, property, retail, healthcare, professional services and logistics.

Smart AI Solutions Education is our training arm. Live, guided, hands-on programmes that take people from cautious to capable, and give organisations a route from AI awareness to AI that is actually part of the working week.

What makes it different
  • Live and guided, never a video library. Every session is delivered in real time with demonstrations, guided activities and open question time.
  • Hands-on from session one. Delegates work in the tools on their own laptop, on their own real tasks, not on a case study about someone else's business.

Subjects we cover

Ten sessions, from
“what is AI” to building agents.

Every programme runs the same ten-session curriculum. Sessions one to five build the foundation; six to ten turn it into output.

1

AI in Action

A live demonstration where one idea becomes research, marketing copy, social posts, a blog, a website, a video, a presentation and an infographic, in front of you, in real time.

2

AI Foundations

How we got here, the difference between narrow and general AI, machine learning, deep learning and natural language processing, how large language models are trained, and why training data shapes everything.

3

Prompt Engineering

The single skill that multiplies every other tool. Structured prompting frameworks, cross-prompting between models, extracting and encoding your brand voice, and the interview technique that gets AI to draw ideas out of you.

4

Privacy, Ethics, POPIA and Risk

What never to paste into a public AI tool, what free tools do with your inputs, bias and accountability, how POPIA and the national AI policy framework apply, and how to draft an AI use policy for your business.

5

Advanced ChatGPT & LLM Workflows

Beyond chatting: personas and custom instructions, custom GPTs, projects, voice, canvas and connectors, deep research, working across Excel and documents, and when to reach for a different model instead.

6

AI at Work & Your Adoption Plan

Mapping your current workflows, finding your leverage points, and writing a personal adoption plan you can start on Monday. Includes Microsoft Copilot across Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint and the browser.

7

AI Design & Video Production

Generating and editing graphics, social and campaign collateral, image generation from text, video generation and editing, animated characters, AI voiceover and AI music, then final assembly.

8

AI-Powered Marketing

Deep market and competitor research, audience and persona prompts, a month of social content from a single structured prompt, content calendars, cross-platform repurposing, lead discovery, and writing for both humans and AI search.

9

Building With AI

Websites and web apps generated from prompts, AI-assisted code, no-code and low-code build tools, and the real difference between a language model, an automation and an autonomous agent.

10

Certification Paths & What Next

Where to pursue recognised international AI certification, the resource hubs worth following in a field that moves weekly, your full resource pack, and entry into the alumni community.
